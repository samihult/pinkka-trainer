Fostering a deeper connection with the biological world.

The Living Archive isn't just a database. It's an educational platform built on the belief that precision leads to appreciation. By documenting the minute details of the species around us, we create a record that is both scientifically accurate and aesthetically profound.

Our mission is to provide institutional-grade resources to students, researchers, and citizen scientists, ensuring that knowledge of our planet’s biodiversity remains accessible, curated, and beautiful.
