Specimen 042 // Sanikkaiset

Pteridium aquilinum

Commonly known as Eagle Fern or Bracken.

Taxonomy & Data

Morphology

Frond Structure

Large, highly divided fronds, often reaching 1-3 meters in length. Tripinnate architecture.

Reproduction

Spores produced in marginal sori, protected by a continuous false indusium.

Ecological Niche

light_mode Full Sun
water_drop Well Drained

A cosmopolitan species occurring in temperate and subtropical regions of both hemispheres. Often colonizes disturbed sites and woodland edges.

psychology Memory Anchor

"Bracken has 'wings' - look for the eagle-like shape of its fronds when viewed from above."
